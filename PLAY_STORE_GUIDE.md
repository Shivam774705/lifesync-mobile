## 2. Can You Upload It to the Google Play Store?
✅ **YES!** LifeSync uses **Trusted Web Activity (TWA)** — Google's official standard to package web applications into an Android `.aab` / `.apk` for the Play Store.

### 💰 Google Play Store Fee:
- **Google Play Store charge**: Google requires a **one-time $25 (approx. ₹2,100) registration fee** for a Google Play Console Developer Account.
- There are **no monthly or yearly fees** after that. Once you pay $25 once, you can upload **unlimited apps for your entire life**.
- If you don't want to pay the $25 right now, you can still install and share the **free .apk directly** with anyone!

## 3. How to Turn LifeSync into an APK / Play Store App (Free Cloud Tools):

### Method A: PWABuilder (Microsoft's Free Tool — Easiest & 100% Free)
1. Upload your code to a free **GitHub repository**.
2. Enable **GitHub Pages** (Settings &rarr; Pages &rarr; Source: `main` &rarr; Save).
3. Open **[pwabuilder.com](https://www.pwabuilder.com/)**.
4. Enter your GitHub Pages URL (e.g., `https://yourusername.github.io/task-tracker`).
5. Click **"Package for Stores"** &rarr; Select **Android**.
6. PWABuilder will automatically generate:
   - **`app-release.apk`**: For installing directly on your Android mobile.
   - **`app-release.aab`**: Ready to upload straight into Google Play Console!
7. **Cost**: **$0 (100% Free forever)**.
